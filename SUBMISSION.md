**Source code:** https://github.com/Wjaas/study-room-booker

**Description:** Common Room is a study room booking prototype. Users can browse rooms by date, time, capacity, and features, then create a booking. They can read their bookings, edit dates and times, or cancel them. Rooms can also be created, viewed, edited, and deleted. The app prevents overlapping bookings in the same room and saves data in the browser.

**How I used AI and tested it:** I used ChatGPT Codex to plan the app, develop the interface and booking logic, and write tests. I tested overlapping and adjacent bookings, editing an existing booking, capacity limits, minimum duration, and past-date rejection. I also checked room and booking CRUD and reload persistence in a browser, then verified that the published page and scripts load. This is a browser-local prototype, so different visitors do not share bookings.
