**Description:** Common Room is a study room booking prototype. Users can browse rooms by date, time, capacity, and features, then create a booking. They can read their bookings, edit dates and times, or cancel them. Rooms can also be created, viewed, edited, and deleted. The app prevents overlapping bookings in the same room and saves data in the browser.

**How I used AI and tested it:** I used ChatGPT Codex to plan the app, develop the interface and booking logic, and write tests. I tested overlapping and adjacent bookings, editing an existing booking, capacity limits, minimum duration, invalid dates, and past-date rejection. I checked room and booking CRUD, conflict messages, and reload persistence in a browser, then verified the public demo and source links. This is a browser-local prototype, so different visitors do not share bookings.
